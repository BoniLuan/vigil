# ADR 0006: v0.1 state, retention, and uptime semantics

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Decision

Service states are `pending`, `up`, `down`, and `paused`. Pausing does not
resolve an open incident. Do not add `degraded` without a concrete semantic
requirement.

For fixed rolling windows of 24 hours, 7, 30, and 90 days, uptime is:

```text
successful completed checks / all completed checks
```

Timeout, DNS, connection, TLS, and unexpected HTTP-status outcomes are failures.
Pending and paused periods/checks are excluded. This check-ratio is not a
time-weighted availability calculation.

Retain check results for 90 days, Prometheus metrics for 30 days, and incidents
indefinitely initially. Retention should be configurable where practical. Do
not partition tables in v0.1.

## Consequences

Uptime is transparent and inexpensive to explain, but check frequency affects
its weighting. Retention cleanup will be required before accumulated check data
exceeds 90 days. Future semantic changes require an ADR and careful API/UI
labeling.
